## Purpose

NetFlow Sentinel v2.0 studies multi-source network-flow anomaly detection, cross-domain shift and an evidence-linked traceability architecture on a hybrid desktop-sidecar platform. The public website is a static research portal; it does not run a detector, capture packets or monitor live traffic.

Version `2.0.0` completes a hard cutover from the v1 `NF-UNSW-NB15-v3` baseline (archived under `historical/`) to the **`canonical-flow-v2-18`** contract (6 base + 12 derived flow features). It integrates three active v2 datasets (**`AIT-NDS-v2`**, **`CTU-SME-11`** and evaluation-only **`UWF-ZeekData24`**), a scenario-disjoint split (`v2-scenario-group-purged-v1`), **four frozen `E0` baseline models** (`Random Forest`, `XGBoost`, `Isolation Forest` and `Autoencoder`) plus `E1` multi-domain and Stage-2 6-class taxonomy extensions, a fail-closed `"deployment"` OOD gate, a 16-D behavioral session engine, a 0–100 composite risk engine, `AES-256-GCM` + Windows `DPAPI` + Adaptive Key Rotation cryptography, a pure-Rust NetFlow v9/IPFIX collector (`telemetry/netflow-rs/`), and a **Tauri 2 (`2.0.0`) + Svelte 5 + FastAPI (`0.1.0`) + SQLite** desktop workstation supporting three operational modes:

1. **Mode 1 — Offline CSV Analysis**: Batch upload and SHA-256-verified scoring of canonical 18-feature CSV slices.
2. **Mode 2 — Dataset Replay**: Controlled streaming replay (`399.44 flow/s` median throughput) through `LiveStreamOrchestrator`.
3. **Mode 3 — Live Lab Monitoring**: Real-time UDP NetFlow v9 (RFC 3954) / IPFIX (RFC 7011) ingestion via the pure-Rust collector or Python UDP receiver.

## Seven core modules

- **Data & Provenance Layer**: Multi-source ingestion (`AIT-NDS-v2`, `CTU-SME-11`, `UWF-ZeekData24`), anti-imputation contract (`FEATURE_INCOMPATIBLE`) and scenario-disjoint split manifest.
- **Train-Only Preprocessing**: `TrainOnlyPreprocessor` with `asinh_robust` scaling and `MIN_BOUNDED_SCALE_FLOOR = 0.25`.
- **Dual-Stage ML & OOD Gate**: 4 `E0` baselines (`4/4 RESEARCH_ONLY`), `E1` multi-domain models, Stage-2 6-class classifier (`Macro-F1 = 0.7347`), `OODValidator`, `OODRejectionGate` and `OOD_AWARE_ENSEMBLE` with `ABSTAINED_OOD` abstention.
- **Behavioral Engine**: 16-D session window aggregation (`src_ip`, 5-minute tumbling window) and multi-flow sequence tracking.
- **Composite Risk & Dry-Run Response**: Deterministic 0–100 risk scoring and `ResponsePolicy` locked at `dry_run = True` (`executed = false`).
- **Cryptographic Telemetry & Key Lifecycle**: `AES-256-GCM` AEAD, `NonceRegistry`, Windows `DPAPI` keystore and Adaptive Key Rotation (`RQ5`/`RQ6`).
- **Desktop Workstation & Localhost Sidecar**: Tauri 2 + Svelte 5 7-panel UI coupled to a loopback FastAPI (`127.0.0.1`) + SQLite backend (`33` paths / `35` operations) protected by `X-Sentinel-Session-Token`.

## Evidence levels and legal boundaries

Every claim is classified as **PLANNED**, **IMPLEMENTED**, **TESTED**, **EXPERIMENTALLY VALIDATED** or **DEFERRED**. All four `E0` models hold tier **`RESEARCH_ONLY`** (`cross_source_validated = false`) due to zero-shot domain shift on `CTU-SME-11`. `AIT-LDS-v2.1` (`CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`) is strictly isolated in `.gitignored` storage, `UGR'16` remains **`BLOCKED`** (`HTTP 403`), and public binary distribution remains locked (`DISTRIBUTION_AUTHORIZATION.json: approved = false`).
