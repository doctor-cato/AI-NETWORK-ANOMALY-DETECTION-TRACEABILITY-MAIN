## Honest distinction between unit tests, localhost replay and physical labs

The audit reports in [docs/audits/PR156_HEAD_AUDIT_2026-11-19.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/docs/audits/PR156_HEAD_AUDIT_2026-11-19.md) and [reports/audits/V2_FINAL_AUDIT.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/reports/audits/V2_FINAL_AUDIT.md) enforce strict verification semantics:

- Passing constructor tests or synthetic fixtures verifies **code correctness (`TESTED`)**, not real-dataset performance (`EXPERIMENTALLY VALIDATED`).
- Localhost UDP packet replay verifies the NetFlow v9/IPFIX parser and streaming pipeline, but **does not substitute for physical hardware switch/router export (`real_device_tested = false`)**.
- Any dataset check that exits `0` when optional raw archives are absent is reported honestly as **`SKIPPED`**, never as a real-data `PASS`.
