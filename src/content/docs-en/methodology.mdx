## Scenario-disjoint split (`v2-scenario-group-purged-v1`)

To eliminate within-scenario temporal and topological leakage, [data/V2_SPLIT_MANIFEST.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/data/V2_SPLIT_MANIFEST.json) partitions `AIT-NDS-v2` (`3,464,400` labeled flows) by **entire cyber-range scenarios** under contract `v2-scenario-group-purged-v1`:

- **Train (`2,097,891` rows, 5 scenarios)**: `fox`, `harrison`, `russellmitchell`, `santos`, `shaw` (`2,051,908` Benign + `45,983` Attack).
- **Validation (`377,704` rows, 1 scenario)**: `wardbeck` (`368,263` Benign + `9,441` Attack).
- **Held-out Test (`988,805` rows, 2 scenarios)**: `wheeler`, `wilson` (`981,392` Benign + `7,413` Attack).
- **Zero leakage invariant**: **`0` scenario overlap** and **`0` `(5-tuple + timestamp)` overlap** across Train, Validation and Test.

## `TrainOnlyPreprocessor` (`asinh_robust`)

Implemented in [src/network_anomaly/preprocessing/pipeline.py](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/src/network_anomaly/preprocessing/pipeline.py), `TrainOnlyPreprocessor` fits exclusively on the Train partition (and on **benign-only Train** `2,051,908` rows for unsupervised detectors). Heavy-tailed flow counters and rates are transformed via `asinh_robust` scaling (`arcsinh(x)` followed by median/IQR centering) with **`MIN_BOUNDED_SCALE_FLOOR = 0.25`** to prevent near-zero IQR collapse on sparse features from exploding test-time z-scores.

## RQ1–RQ6 research protocol

Full experimental hypotheses and results are documented in [docs/RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/docs/RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md) and [docs/thesis/RESEARCH_REPORT_V2.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/docs/thesis/RESEARCH_REPORT_V2.md):

- **RQ1 (Cross-Source Generalization & OOD Gate)**: Evaluates zero-shot transfer from `AIT-NDS-v2` (`E0`) to `CTU-SME-11` and `UWF-ZeekData24`, plus `E1` multi-domain adaptation and `OODRejectionGate` abstention ([DOMAIN_SHIFT_ANALYSIS.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/reports/experiments/ml/domain_analysis/DOMAIN_SHIFT_ANALYSIS.md)).
- **RQ2 (Severe Class Imbalance & Calibration)**: Compares class weighting, resampling and threshold calibration on `AIT-NDS-v2` ([rq2_imbalance_report.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/reports/experiments/imbalance/rq2_imbalance_report.json)).
- **RQ3 (16-D Behavioral Session Correlation)**: Measures how 5-minute `src_ip` behavioral vectors lift stealthy `Credential_Attack` detection ([rq3_session_correlation_experiment.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/reports/experiments/behavioral/rq3_session_correlation_experiment.json)).
- **RQ4 (Leave-One-Attack-Family-Out Zero-Day Detection)**: Benchmarks unsupervised `Isolation Forest` and `Autoencoder` on unseen attack families ([rq4_unknown_attack_report.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/reports/experiments/ml/rq4_unknown_attack_report.json)).
- **RQ5 & RQ6 (Adaptive Key Rotation & AEAD Throughput)**: Quantifies exposure window reduction (`-87.5%`), blast radius reduction (`-85.0%`) and `AES-256-GCM` latency/throughput ([rq5_rotation_experiment.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/reports/experiments/crypto/rq5_rotation_experiment.json), [crypto_benchmark_report.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/36f63081bf30de98b1dc1058a780ae79ae2cf5b3/reports/benchmarks/crypto_benchmark_report.json)).
