## Datasets & published DOIs

- **`AIT-NDS-v2` (Primary v2 Network Flow Dataset, `CC BY 4.0`)**: M. Landauer et al., Austrian Institute of Technology, Zenodo, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.13168643](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13168643).
- **`AIT-LDS-v2.1` (Isolated Host/Log Dataset, `CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`)**: M. Landauer et al., Austrian Institute of Technology, Zenodo, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19483937](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19483937).
- **`AIT-ADS` (Alert Dataset Reference)**: Austrian Institute of Technology, Zenodo, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.8263181](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8263181).
- **`CTU-SME-11` (External Cross-Network Flow Dataset, `CC BY 4.0`)**: Stratosphere Laboratory, Czech Technical University in Prague, Zenodo, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7958258](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7958258).
- **Historical v1 `NF-UNSW-NB15-v3` (Archived in `historical/`)**: University of Queensland eSpace (`UQ 454104`), [DOI 10.48610/6E0EDA1](https://doi.org/10.48610/6E0EDA1); M. Luay et al., [_Temporal Analysis of NetFlow Datasets for Network Intrusion Detection Systems_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04404).
