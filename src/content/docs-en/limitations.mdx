## Scientific & cross-domain limitations

Documented in [docs/thesis/RESEARCH_REPORT_V2.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/docs/thesis/RESEARCH_REPORT_V2.md), [docs/thesis/DEFENSE_QA_V2.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/docs/thesis/DEFENSE_QA_V2.md) and [reports/experiments/ml/domain_analysis/DOMAIN_SHIFT_ANALYSIS.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/reports/experiments/ml/domain_analysis/DOMAIN_SHIFT_ANALYSIS.md):

- **All 4 `E0` models are `RESEARCH_ONLY` (`cross_source_validated = false`)**: While `Random Forest` reaches `F1 = 0.9950` on scenario-disjoint `ait_test`, zero-shot evaluation on external `CTU-SME-11` drops to `F1 = 0.4534` (`RF`) and `0.3405` (`XGB`) before `E1` multi-domain adaptation (`CTU_Test F1 = 0.9564`).
- **Bayes error & identical L4 feature collisions**: Coarse L4 flow summaries can produce identical 18-feature vectors for short benign polling and lightweight probing flows, setting an irreducible Bayes error floor for single-flow classifiers.
- **Single-flow `Credential_Attack` recall (`0.1410`)**: In Stage-2 6-class flow classification ([multiclass_attack_classification_report.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/reports/experiments/ml/multiclass_attack_classification_report.json)), single-flow `Credential_Attack` reaches only `0.1410` recall because individual login attempts resemble benign authentication flows until correlated across 5-minute 16-D behavioral sessions (`RQ3` session F1 `0.9412`).
- **Unsupervised zero-day recall ceiling (`RQ4`)**: Under leave-one-attack-family-out testing ([rq4_unknown_attack_report.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/reports/experiments/ml/rq4_unknown_attack_report.json)), `Autoencoder` achieves mean unseen-family recall of **`0.2045`** (and `Isolation Forest` `0.1306`), showing that L4 volumetric anomalies alone cannot catch low-rate semantic attacks.

## Dataset, linkage & legal boundaries

- **No direct flow-to-host UID join (`DIRECT_UID_LINK = FALSE`)**: As verified in [docs/dataset/AIT_LINKAGE_FEASIBILITY.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/docs/dataset/AIT_LINKAGE_FEASIBILITY.md), `72.87–87.91%` of timestamp windows in `AIT` exhibit multi-flow ambiguity.
- **`AIT-LDS-v2.1` (`CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`) isolation & `UWF-ZeekData24` (`evaluation_only = true`)**: `AIT-LDS-v2.1` is strictly isolated in `.gitignored` storage; `UWF-ZeekData24` is restricted to evaluation only; and **`UGR'16` remains `BLOCKED` (`HTTP 403`)** ([reports/audits/V2_UGR16_ALTERNATIVES_REVIEW.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/reports/audits/V2_UGR16_ALTERNATIVES_REVIEW.md)).

## Operational, hardware & distribution gates

- **No live firewall execution**: `ResponsePolicy` is locked at `dry_run = True` (`executed = false`).
- **Physical NetFlow appliance export (`#137`)**: Localhost UDP replay is verified, whereas physical switch/router hardware testing remains **`real_device_tested = false`**.
- **NSIS Clean Windows VM E2E (`#148`) & distribution lock (`#154`)**: Clean-VM NSIS installer attestation is **`DEFERRED`**, and [reports/governance/DISTRIBUTION_AUTHORIZATION.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/reports/governance/DISTRIBUTION_AUTHORIZATION.json) remains **`approved = false`**.
