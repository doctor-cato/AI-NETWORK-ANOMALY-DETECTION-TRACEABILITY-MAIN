## Benchmark validity

The verified package contains flows observed in 2015 and generated in a controlled environment. It is not a sample of current production networks. Duplicate or equivalent vectors, TTL separation and repeated scenarios can inflate Validation confidence. Cross-dataset generalization is not established by the reported Validation scores.

## System readiness

The research portal is static. The intended correlation, risk, incident trace, encrypted storage and dashboard layers are not operational capabilities on the audited `main` branch. Open PRs describe work under review, not merged features.

## Missing evidence

Held-out Test metrics for the four model families, real-data Isolation Forest and Autoencoder comparisons, operational security validation and cross-dataset conclusions are **Not yet verified** in this portal.
