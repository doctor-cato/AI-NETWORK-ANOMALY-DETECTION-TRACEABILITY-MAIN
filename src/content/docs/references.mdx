- **AIT-LDS-v2.1 (AIT Log Data Set v2.1)**: [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19483937](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19483937) (`CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`) — cách ly trong `.gitignored`, chỉ dùng để kiểm toán khả năng liên kết dấu thời gian (`#85`, [docs/dataset/AIT_LINKAGE_FEASIBILITY.md](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/docs/dataset/AIT_LINKAGE_FEASIBILITY.md)).

- **UWF-ZeekData24 (University of West Florida ZeekData24)**: `CC BY 4.0` — chỉ sử dụng để đánh giá ngoại vi (`evaluation_only = true`, [reports/experiments/ml/uwf_zeekdata24_cross_source_eval.json](https://github.com/doctor-cato/AI-NETWORK-ANOMALY-DETECTION-TRACEABILITY-MAIN/blob/18db24e637399d077312e60e4911e569b9b099d2/reports/experiments/ml/uwf_zeekdata24_cross_source_eval.json)).
